TRRRST Watch is a minimal, offline companion for the Casio F-B100W (module 3589).

Synchronize time, download steps and distance, configure alarms and the hourly time signal, manage home and world-time cities, and change watch settings such as display format, key sounds, power saving, backlight, connection time, profile, and timer.

All data stays on your phone. The app has no account, cloud synchronization, analytics, ads, proprietary dependencies, or INTERNET permission.

This is an independent, unofficial project and is not affiliated with CASIO.
